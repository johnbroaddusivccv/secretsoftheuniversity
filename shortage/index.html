    U.S. overdose deaths by drug involved. A death can involve more than one drug, so rows overlap.

    Combinations that drive the risk

    • Fentanyl + stimulants: cocaine or meth cut with fentanyl. Behind 43% of overdose deaths, 2021–24.
    • Opioids + xylazine: a vet sedative naloxone can't reverse. Rose from 3% to 11% of fentanyl deaths, 2019–22.
    • Opioids + benzos or alcohol: each slows breathing, and together the effect compounds.
    • Counterfeit pills: look like prescriptions but may hold fentanyl. Deaths involving them more than doubled, 2019–21.

    FAQs

    Common questions

    Does Broaddus Scientific Group dispense medications?

    No. We do not manufacture, dispense or take title to any drug. Licensed third-party pharmacies and outsourcing facilities fill and ship every order, within the scope of their state licenses.

    Who can become a member?

    Licensed prescribers, clinics, telehealth companies and health organizations. We verify licenses and credentials before ordering is turned on.

    What is the difference between 503A and 503B?

    503A pharmacies compound for individual patients against a prescription and are overseen mainly by state boards. 503B outsourcing facilities register with the FDA, follow current good manufacturing practice, and may compound in bulk without patient-specific prescriptions.

    How is the admin fee disclosed?

    The fee percentage is written into your member agreement and shown on every contract price, so you can see what you pay and why.

    Can you help when a drug is on the shortage list?

    Often, yes. Depending on the item, a partner may offer an alternative strength, a different presentation, or a compounded version permitted while the drug is in shortage. Use Shortage Watch to send a request.

    Where do you ship?

    Orders ship within the United States only, from facilities licensed for the destination state.
