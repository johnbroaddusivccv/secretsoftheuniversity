Reading the status column — Rx: FDA-approved, legal only with a prescription filled at a licensed U.S. pharmacy; ordering from overseas sites without one is illegal importation, and modafinil and armodafinil are DEA Schedule IV. Schedule I: no lawful retail channel. Not approved: no FDA-approved product; sold as "research use only", which means not for human consumption. Gray area: sold as a supplement, but the ingredient's status is unsettled. Dietary supplement: lawful to sell; not FDA-reviewed for effectiveness. Status is as of October 2026. This index is reference only and not medical advice; dosing is deliberately left out, and several compounds interact with prescription drugs (methylene blue is an MAO inhibitor; tadalafil must never be combined with nitrates).

Formulas and masses are reference values for the free peptide (acetate salts weigh more); sequences use one-letter codes where the peptide is unmodified.
