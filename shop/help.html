Ordering & payment

Open a product, choose a size and quantity, and add it to your cart. At checkout, enter your contact and shipping details and place the order.

No card is charged on the site. We review each order and email you payment instructions together with the current lot’s certificate of analysis. Your items are held until payment is complete. Every order gets a number in the format BSG-XXXXX-XXXX for reference.

Shipping & delivery

United States. Standard shipping is $12, and free on orders of $150 or more. Express (2 business days) is $25, or $15 on orders of $150 or more.

Outside the US. We ship to select countries. Shipping is quoted by email before you pay. You are responsible for following the import rules where you live.

Dispatch and tracking. Orders paid before the daily cutoff ship the same business day. Peptides ship cold-packed where needed. Tracking is emailed when your order leaves us.

Refunds & returns

Because these are sensitive research materials, opened products can’t be returned. If something arrives damaged, incorrect or missing, or a certificate of analysis doesn’t match your lot, email us within 14 days of delivery with your order number and photos. We’ll replace the item or refund it.

If a package is lost in transit, contact us once tracking has stopped updating for 7 days and we’ll work it out with you.

Testing & certificates of analysis

Every order ships with a certificate of analysis (COA) for its lot. A COA lists the product’s identity, purity and lot number, as measured by an independent laboratory.

Peptides are produced to a purity standard of 99% or higher, confirmed by HPLC for purity and mass spectrometry for identity. For other compounds, the purity of each lot is printed on its COA.

Want to see the COA before you order? Email us with the product name and we’ll send the current lot’s certificate.

Frequently asked questions

Are these products for human use?

No. Everything is sold strictly for laboratory research use. These products are not drugs, supplements, foods or cosmetics, and are not for human or veterinary use.

How should I store what I order?

Each product page lists storage under Specifications. In general: keep powders sealed, cool and dry; refrigerate solutions and sprays; store lyophilized peptides frozen at −20 °C and refrigerate them once reconstituted.

What do the solution concentrations mean?

A solution listed as 10mg/mL contains 10 milligrams of the compound in each milliliter of liquid. The bottle size is on the label and the COA.

What do Backorder and 1–3 day delay mean?

Backorder items can be pre-ordered and ship as soon as stock arrives. A 1–3 day delay means the item is in stock but needs a little longer to pack.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Yes, until it has been paid and shipped. Email us with your order number.
