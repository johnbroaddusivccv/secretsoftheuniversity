Ordering & Payment

How do I place an order?

Browse the catalog or glossary, open a product, choose a size, and add it to your cart. When you're ready, open the cart and proceed to checkout to enter your shipping details and place the order.

Card checkout is being finalized, so no charge is taken at checkout today. When you place an order it's recorded and reserved, and our team follows up by email with secure payment instructions to complete it. You'll receive an order number (format BSG-XXXXX-XXXX) for reference.

Do you offer discount codes?

Yes — enter a valid code in the discount field at checkout and the total updates automatically. Codes may include percentage discounts, minimum-order offers, or free shipping.

Can I look up an order I placed?

Yes. Use the Order Lookup page and enter your order number to view its status and details.

Shipping

How much is shipping?

Standard shipping is a flat rate and is free on orders over $150. Express (2-day) cold-chain shipping is available at checkout for a higher rate. Exact costs are shown in your order summary before you place the order.

How are products packaged and shipped?

Lyophilized compounds are sealed and, where required, shipped cold-chain to preserve integrity. Orders placed before the daily cutoff ship the same business day, and tracking is emailed once your order leaves the facility.

Do you ship internationally?

Select international destinations are supported at checkout. The buyer is responsible for compliance with all import regulations and applicable laws in their jurisdiction.

Products & Documentation

What is a Certificate of Analysis (COA)?

A COA is a batch-specific document listing a compound's purity, identity, molecular weight, and lot number, verified by independent third-party HPLC and mass spectrometry. A COA is included with every order, and you can request the current lot's certificate before purchasing.

What purity standard do you hold?

Compounds are produced to a ≥99% purity standard (a small number of complex proteins are ≥95–98%, noted on the product page), verified per lot by third-party testing.

How should I store and reconstitute compounds?

Store lyophilized material at -20°C; once reconstituted, refrigerate at 2–8°C and use within the window noted on each product's handling tab. Reconstitute with bacteriostatic water — draw volume depends on your target research concentration.

Research Use

Are these products for human use?

No. All products are sold strictly for in-vitro laboratory and research use only. They are not drugs, supplements, foods, or cosmetics, are not for human or veterinary consumption, and nothing on this site is medical advice or a therapeutic claim. By ordering you confirm you are a qualified researcher of legal age.

Are the dosing figures on the site instructions?

No. Any dosing, cycle, or protocol figures are research and educational references compiled from published literature — they are not prescriptions or compounding instructions. Always verify compound identity and purity against the batch COA.

What is your return policy?

Because these are sensitive research materials, orders are reviewed case by case. If there's an issue with your order or documentation, contact us and we'll make it right.
