Quality & Testing

How we verify every batch

Each production lot is analyzed by an independent laboratory. High-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) confirms purity to a ≥99% standard, and mass spectrometry confirms molecular identity and weight. Results are recorded on a batch-specific certificate of analysis tied to the lot number printed on your vial.

Compounds are lyophilized, sealed, and stored under controlled conditions. Orders ship cold-chain where required, with reconstitution supplies available so material arrives ready for laboratory handling. Chain-of-custody documentation is retained per lot.
