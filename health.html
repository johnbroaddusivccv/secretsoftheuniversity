Overview

What are peptides

Short chains of amino acids that act as signaling molecules. Their role in wellness.

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that act as signaling molecules in the body, regulating a variety of biological functions — including tissue repair, metabolism, inflammation, and hormonal balance.

Their ability to target specific cellular pathways makes them invaluable tools in regenerative medicine, anti-aging, and metabolic optimization.

By leveraging peptides, you can achieve enhanced recovery, fat loss, inflammation reduction, and overall improved vitality with minimal side effects.

Targeted signalingAct on specific receptors and pathways rather than broad systemic effects.
Recovery & metabolismSupport tissue repair, fat metabolism, and energy regulation.
Well-toleratedFavorable side-effect profile when dosed responsibly under guidance.
Compound Library

The peptide index

A reference catalog of research peptides and bioactive compounds, organized by category. Hover or tap to expand.

Peptide
Short chains of amino acids (2–50) that act as signaling molecules, telling cells to perform specific tasks — repair, regulate, or modulate.
Agonist
A substance that binds to a cell receptor and activates it to produce a biological response. Example: semaglutide (GLP-1 agonist).
Bioavailability
The proportion of a compound that reaches systemic circulation after administration. Route of delivery matters.
Reconstitution
Adding a solvent (typically bacteriostatic water) to a lyophilized (freeze-dried) peptide powder to prepare it for use.
Half-life
The time required for the concentration of a compound to fall to half its initial value in the body.
Lyophilized
Freeze-dried. Most research peptides ship in lyophilized form and require reconstitution before use.
Metabolic / GLP-1

Semaglutide

GLP-1 receptor agonist
Mimics the incretin hormone GLP-1. Studied extensively for appetite regulation, glucose balance, and weight management.

Acts on the hypothalamus to increase satiety signaling. Slows gastric emptying. Originally developed for type 2 diabetes; now one of the most-studied compounds in metabolic medicine.

PubMed → semaglutide
Metabolic / GLP-1

Retatrutide

Triple agonist: GLP-1 / GIP / glucagon
Next-generation multi-receptor agonist studied for enhanced fat metabolism, appetite regulation, and glucose control.

Engages three receptors simultaneously — a first in its class. Phase 2 trials showed significant body weight reduction. Investigational; not yet FDA-approved.

PubMed → retatrutide
Metabolic / GLP-1

Tirzepatide

Dual agonist: GLP-1 / GIP
Dual incretin receptor agonist studied for glycemic control and weight management. FDA-approved for type 2 diabetes.

Acts on both GLP-1 and GIP receptors. Shown to produce substantial weight loss in clinical trials. Marketed as Mounjaro for diabetes and Zepbound for weight management.

PubMed → tirzepatide
Metabolic

AOD-9604

Anti-obesity drug fragment 9604
A modified fragment of human growth hormone (hGH 176-191) studied for its effects on fat metabolism without impacting blood sugar or growth.

Stimulates lipolysis (fat breakdown) and inhibits lipogenesis (fat accumulation). Does not affect IGF-1 levels. Research-use compound.

PubMed → AOD-9604
Metabolic

MOTS-c

Mitochondrial-derived peptide
A mitochondrial-derived peptide that targets the folate-methionine cycle, studied for metabolic regulation and exercise mimicry.

Activates AMPK pathway. Research suggests it may improve insulin sensitivity and promote cellular energy balance. Sometimes called an "exercise mimetic."

PubMed → MOTS-c
Recovery / Regenerative

BPC-157

Body Protection Compound-157
A pentadecapeptide derived from human gastric juice. One of the most-studied compounds in regenerative peptide research.

Extensive preclinical research on tissue repair (tendons, ligaments, muscles), gastrointestinal protection, and modulation of nitric oxide synthesis. Research-use compound.

PubMed → BPC-157
Recovery / Regenerative

TB-500

Thymosin Beta-4 fragment
A synthetic fragment of naturally occurring thymosin beta-4. Studied for cell migration, angiogenesis, and soft tissue repair.

Promotes wound healing and new blood vessel formation in preclinical models. Often studied in combination with BPC-157 for synergistic recovery effects.

PubMed → thymosin beta-4
Recovery / Regenerative

Pentadecapeptide (BPC Blend)

BPC-157 + TB-500 combination
A blend of the two most-studied regenerative peptides. Investigated for complementary mechanisms in tissue repair and recovery.

Combines BPC-157's gastrointestinal and tendon support with TB-500's angiogenic and cell-migration properties. Popular in recovery research protocols.

Growth Hormone

CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin

GHRH analog + GH secretagogue
A combination of a growth hormone-releasing hormone analog and a selective ghrelin receptor agonist. Studied for pulsatile GH release.

CJC-1295 extends the half-life of endogenous GHRH. Ipamorelin stimulates GH release without significantly affecting cortisol or prolactin. Often dosed at bedtime to mimic natural GH pulsation.

PubMed → CJC-1295
Growth Hormone

Tesamorelin + Ipamorelin

GHRH analog blend
Tesamorelin is an FDA-approved GHRH analog (for lipodystrophy). Combined with Ipamorelin for enhanced GH secretion research.

Tesamorelin is one of the few peptides with FDA approval (Egrifta). The combination is studied for body composition optimization and visceral fat reduction.

PubMed → tesamorelin
Growth Hormone

Hexarelin

GH secretagogue hexapeptide
A potent growth hormone secretagogue. Studied for its effects on GH release, cardiac function, and body composition.

One of the strongest GH-releasing peptides by molar potency. Also studied for cardioprotective effects independent of GH release. Research-use compound.

PubMed → hexarelin
Immune

Thymosin Alpha-1

Tα1 · immune modulator
A naturally occurring thymic peptide that modulates T-cell function. Approved in some countries for hepatitis B and as an immune adjuvant.

Enhances dendritic cell function and T-cell maturation. One of the few peptides with international regulatory approvals (Zadaxin). Studied for immune support in chronic infections.

PubMed → thymosin alpha-1
Immune / Anti-inflammatory

KPV

Alpha-MSH fragment (Lys-Pro-Val)
A tripeptide fragment of alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone studied for anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties.

Research focuses on gut inflammation, mucosal healing, and immune modulation. Investigated as a targeted anti-inflammatory without systemic immunosuppression.

PubMed → KPV
Cognitive / Anxiolytic

Selank

Synthetic tuftsin analog
A synthetic heptapeptide analog of tuftsin, studied for anxiolytic and nootropic effects. Approved in Russia for anxiety disorders.

Modulates GABA, serotonin, and dopamine systems. Does not produce sedation or dependence in preclinical models. Administered intranasally in approved formulations.

PubMed → selank
Cognitive / Neuroprotective

Semax

Synthetic ACTH(4-10) analog
A neuropeptide derived from ACTH. Studied for cognitive enhancement, neuroprotection, and BDNF modulation.

Increases brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF). Approved in Russia for stroke recovery and cognitive disorders. Intranasal administration.

PubMed → semax
Longevity / Cellular

NAD+

Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide
A coenzyme central to metabolism, DNA repair, and sirtuin activation. Studied extensively in aging and longevity research.

Declines with age. Supplementation studied for mitochondrial function, cognitive clarity, and cellular energy. Available as IV, subcutaneous, and oral (NMN/NR precursors).

PubMed → NAD+
Longevity / Telomere

Epitalon

Epithalon · Ala-Glu-Asp-Gly
A tetrapeptide studied for telomerase activation and pineal gland function. One of the earliest peptides researched for lifespan extension.

Based on the work of Professor Vladimir Khavinson. Studied for its ability to reactivate telomerase in somatic cells and regulate melatonin production. Research-use compound.

PubMed → epitalon
Longevity / Endocrine

PT-141 (Bremelanotide)

Melanocortin receptor agonist
A melanocortin agonist FDA-approved (as Vyleesi) for hypoactive sexual desire disorder. Acts centrally, not peripherally.

Binds MC3R and MC4R receptors in the hypothalamus. One of the few peptides with full FDA approval for a specific indication. Subcutaneous administration.

PubMed → bremelanotide
Copper Peptides

GHK-Cu

Copper tripeptide-1
A naturally occurring copper complex (Gly-His-Lys·Cu²⁺) found in plasma and saliva. Studied for wound healing, collagen synthesis, and skin remodeling.

Promotes collagen and elastin production, attracts immune cells to injury sites, and has antioxidant properties. Available topically and as injectable research compound.

PubMed → GHK-Cu
Copper Peptides

AHK-Cu

Copper tripeptide (Ala-His-Lys·Cu²⁺)
A copper-binding tripeptide studied for hair follicle stimulation and wound repair, often compared with GHK-Cu.

Research suggests it may promote dermal papilla cell proliferation. Less studied than GHK-Cu but gaining interest in cosmetic and regenerative research.
